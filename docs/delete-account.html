Delete Your Cadence Account

Last updated: September 23, 2026

You can permanently delete your Cadence account and all associated data at any time. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

How to delete your account (in-app)

  1. Open the Cadence app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab, then tap Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

If you can't access the app

If you no longer have access to the app or your device, you can request deletion by emailing xati2000@live.com from the email address associated with your account. Please include "Account Deletion Request" in the subject line. We'll process your request within a reasonable timeframe and confirm once it's complete.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, the following is permanently removed:

  • Your profile (name, email, account details)
  • All posts, comments, and likes you've made
  • Your follows and followers
  • All direct messages and message threads you're part of
  • Your notifications
  • Your practice history and progress data (stored locally on your device, removed on sign-out/uninstall)

What may be retained

We do not retain personal data after account deletion, except where required by law or for legitimate safety and moderation records — for example, records related to a report of abuse involving your account may be retained for a limited period as described in our Privacy Policy.

Questions

If you have questions about account deletion or data retention, contact us at xati2000@live.com.
