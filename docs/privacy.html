Privacy Policy — Cadence

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cadence ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects your information. We built Cadence to be a low-pressure, judgment-free space, and we take that seriously when it comes to your data too.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: email address (or sign-in method, e.g. Google), display name, and any profile details you choose to add.

Content you create: posts, comments, daily practice reflections, direct messages, likes, and follows.

Usage information: which situations you practice, your daily reminder settings, and basic app-interaction data needed to make features work (for example, tracking which notifications you've read).

Device information: basic technical information such as device type and OS version, used for app functionality (like scheduling local notifications) and troubleshooting.

We do not currently use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. If this changes in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy before doing so.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide core app functionality (practice tracking, community feed, messaging, notifications)
  • Maintain the safety of the community (enforcing blocks, reviewing reports)
  • Send you daily reminder notifications, if you enable them
  • Improve and maintain the App

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. How Information Is Shared

  • With other users, as intended by the feature: your profile, posts, and comments are visible according to the app's normal community features. Direct messages are visible only to you and the recipient. Blocking a user restricts what they can see of you and vice versa.
  • With service providers: we use backend infrastructure providers (such as our database and authentication provider) to operate the App. These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.
  • For legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users or the public.

4. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate safety/moderation records (for example, records related to a report of abuse may be retained longer).

5. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at xati2000@live.com. If you are a resident of the European Economic Area, the UK, or California, additional rights may apply to you under GDPR/UK GDPR or the CCPA respectively, including the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

6. Children's Privacy

Cadence is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including access controls at the database level restricting who can read your private data (such as direct messages). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International Users

Cadence is available to users worldwide. Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than your own, including the country where our backend infrastructure providers operate. By using the App, you consent to this transfer.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify users (such as an in-app notice) before the changes take effect.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us at: xati2000@live.com
